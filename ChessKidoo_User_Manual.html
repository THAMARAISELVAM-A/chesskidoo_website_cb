1. Introduction

ChessKidoo is an all-in-one platform for running a chess academy. It brings the website, four role-based portals, live classes, homework, a Stockfish-powered PGN analysis lab, tournaments, fees and learning resources into one place. This manual explains, step by step, what each type of user can do.

How to read this manual Buttons you tap are shown like this: ▶ Start. Numbered lists are exact step-by-step actions. Blue boxes are notes, green boxes are tips, red boxes are warnings. Use the left menu to jump to any section.

2. Roles & access

Everyone signs in with the same login screen; what you see is decided by your role.

👨‍💼 Admin

Runs the academy: enrols students, manages logins & roles, classes, timetable, fees, resources, tournaments and analytics.

👨‍🏫 Coach

Teaches: assigns homework, runs live classes, uploads learning materials, assigns/ tracks puzzles, writes reports, manages their schedule.

🎓 Student

Learns: joins live classes, does homework & puzzles, analyses games in the PGN Lab, tracks progress, downloads the report card & certificates.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent

Monitors: views the child's progress, attendance, reports, fees, schedule and gives feedback — updated in real time.
Real-time Most data syncs live. When a coach assigns homework or uploads a resource, it appears in the student's portal within seconds — no refresh needed.

3. Logging in

  1. Open the ChessKidoo website and click Log In in the top menu.
  2. Enter the email and password the academy gave you.
  3. You are taken straight to your portal (Admin / Coach / Student / Parent) based on your role.
  4. To sign out, use the menu in your portal header.
Forgot password? Contact the academy — an Admin can reset any login from the Access Manager.
Screenshot slot: the login screen. (See §14 for how to add real screenshots.)

4. Website & enrolment (for new families)

The public website lets new families learn about the academy and book a free demo.

Book a free demo class

  1. On the home page click Book Free Demo (or any "Explore Course" button).
  2. Fill in the parent's name and the child's age.
  3. Pick your country code from the dropdown (auto-detected) and type the WhatsApp number.
  4. Choose your Country; the city is optional.
  5. Click Book Free Demo — you'll be handed to WhatsApp/email to confirm, and the academy receives the lead.
International friendly The pricing section has a 🌐 Language and 💱 Currency switcher, and prices convert automatically — handy for families abroad.

5. Admin portal

The control centre for the academy. Use the left sidebar to move between sections.

Enrol a new student

  1. Go to the students/dashboard area and click + Enroll.
  2. Fill in name, parent phone, level, batch, coach and fee details.
  3. Click Confirm Enrollment. The student record is created.
  4. Give the student a login from the Access Manager.

6. Coach portal

Everything a coach needs to teach and track students.

Assign homework

  1. Open 🎓 Classroom → 📋 Homework tab.
  2. Enter a title, paste the PGN, choose a type (Study / Guess Move / Practice).
  3. In Assign To pick a specific student (the list shows your real students) or All Students.
  4. Set an optional due date and description, then click 📝 Assign Homework.
Instant delivery The moment you assign, it appears in that student's Homework section (and their nav badge updates) — live.

See submissions & grades

Open the Grades tab. For each assignment you see which students submitted (by name), their accuracy, moves studied and notes — updated live as students submit.

Run a live session

  1. Open Classroom → 📡 Live Class tab.
  2. The Google Meet link is auto-filled from your class room (or generated if missing).
  3. Optionally paste a PGN to broadcast a position, then start the session.
  4. Students see your live board + engine eval + your notes, and a big Join Google Meet button.

Upload learning resources / e-library

  1. Open the 📚 Resources section.
  2. Click + Upload.
  3. Choose File (PDF, etc.) or Link, add a title, level and batch.
  4. Publish — it appears instantly in your students' Learning Resources → Coach Assignments tab.

Assign & track puzzles

Open 🧩 Puzzles. Use 🎯 Assign & Track to give puzzles to students, and the Student Puzzle Progress card to see every puzzle each of your students has solved (live from the database).

7. Student portal

Your learning home. The sidebar holds every tool.

Homework

  1. Click 📒 Homework in the sidebar (a badge shows pending count).
  2. Each card shows the coach, type, due date and a status (⏳ Pending / ✓ Done).
  3. Click ▶ Start to open it on the board, study/solve, then ✓ Submit Homework.
  4. Use ⬇ PGN on any card to download it, or ⬇ Download all (PGN) for everything.

My Puzzles

  1. Click 🧩 My Puzzles. Filter by All / Easy / Medium / Hard.
  2. Pick a puzzle and drag the best move on the board; the opponent replies automatically.
  3. Solve cleanly for a 🌟 Perfect — build a streak for 🔥 bonus praise and confetti!
  4. Solved puzzles are saved, so your progress and "Completed" count persist.

PGN Stockfish Lab

Analyse any game with the engine. See the deep-dive for full detail.

  • Paste a PGN and click 🔬 Analyze PGN, or open a famous game from the library.
  • Step through moves; the evaluation bar and last-move highlight follow along.
  • Import your own games from Lichess or Chess.com into My Games Vault.
  • Save a position with 🗄️ Save to Vault.

Live classroom

  1. Open 🎓 My Classroom → 📡 Live Class.
  2. When class is live, a Join Google Meet card appears — tap it for video.
  3. The board below mirrors the coach's position with live engine eval and notes.

Report card

  1. Open 📊 Report Card.
  2. Use 👁 View to open a clean printable version, ⬇ Download PDF to save it, or 🖨 Print.

The report shows your rating, competency rings (openings, tactics, endgame, etc.), a rating chart and your coach's assessment.

10. PGN Stockfish Lab — deep-dive

The Lab is where games are studied with engine help. It uses a hybrid model: your games are stored locally/in the academy cloud, while master games and player data are pulled from Lichess & Chess.com.

What you can do

  • Analyze: paste a PGN → 🔬 Analyze PGN; step with ⏮ ◀ ▶ ⏭; the eval bar & last-move arrow follow.
  • Famous Games Library: 30+ curated master games by level, plus live Lichess/Chess.com import.
  • My Games Vault: import your Lichess/Chess.com games in one click; search, filter (win/loss/draw, source, colour) and see win-rate & best/worst opening analytics.
  • Save & report: 🗄️ Save to Vault stores a game; the academy can run server-side Stockfish to produce accuracy/blunder reports.
Activating server analysis The cloud Stockfish worker (analyze-pgn) must be deployed by the academy for full engine reports. Until then, the Lab still analyses live in your browser and stores your games.

11. AI Coach Tom

Tom is the in-app assistant. Today he answers common questions (classes, fees, payments, how features work) and surfaces game stats. Ask him from the chat bubble.

Be aware Tom is currently a guided assistant, not a full AI engine-coach. For move-by-move "why was this bad?" explanations, use the PGN Lab with the engine. (An LLM upgrade is on the roadmap.)

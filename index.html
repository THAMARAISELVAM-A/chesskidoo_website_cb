ChessKidoo Academy — Policies

Clear, transparent rules that keep our academy fair, safe, and professional for every student, parent, and coach.

💰 Financial & Accounts Policy

  • All academy fees must be paid on or before the due date.
  • Official receipts will be issued for all payments.
  • Tournament entry fees are generally non-refundable after registration closes unless cancelled by the organizer.
  • ChessKidoo maintains transparent financial records for academy and event operations.
  • Prize money, medals, trophies, and certificates will be distributed according to the published event regulations.
  • Fee structures are communicated in advance; any revision is announced at least 30 days before it takes effect.
  • Payments are accepted through the academy's official channels only (UPI / bank transfer / academy payment portal). Please do not pay any individual privately.

🔒 Privacy & Data Protection Policy

  • Student and parent information (names, contact details, progress records) is collected only for academy operations and is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
  • Since most of our students are minors, all accounts and communications are managed with parent/guardian consent.
  • Photos or videos of students from classes, events, or tournaments are published on academy pages only with prior parent/guardian consent.
  • Academy portal access is protected by individual credentials; please do not share login details.
  • Parents may request a copy, correction, or deletion of their child's personal data by writing to chesskidoo37@gmail.com.
  • Payment records are retained as required for accounting and statutory compliance.

↩️ Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • Monthly coaching fees, once the month has commenced, are non-refundable; unused classes may be adjusted as make-up sessions.
  • If the academy cancels a class, a make-up class or fee adjustment will always be provided.
  • Tournament entry fees are refunded in full if the event is cancelled by the organizer.
  • Long-term program fees (quarterly/annual) may be refunded on a pro-rata basis for medical or relocation reasons, at the academy's discretion with supporting documents.
  • Refunds, where approved, are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.

📋 Attendance & Class Policy

  • Students are expected to join classes on time — 5 minutes early for online sessions.
  • Please inform the coach or academy office in advance for planned absences.
  • Make-up classes are offered for absences informed at least 4 hours ahead, subject to coach availability.
  • Repeated unexcused absences may affect batch placement and tournament nominations.
  • Class schedules, batch changes, and holiday calendars are announced through official academy channels (portal / WhatsApp).

🤝 Code of Conduct & Fair Play Policy

  • Respect for coaches, teammates, and opponents is mandatory — in the classroom, online, and at tournaments.
  • Any form of chess engine or outside assistance during games, assessments, or online tournaments is strictly prohibited.
  • Students should maintain sportsmanship: shake hands (or greet online), accept results gracefully, and analyse losses as learning.
  • Bullying, abusive language, or disruptive behaviour may lead to suspension from classes or events.
  • Parents are requested to support fair-play values and route concerns through the academy office rather than approaching students or coaches mid-session.

🛡️ Online Class & Child Safety Policy

  • Online classes are conducted on official Google Meet links shared through the academy portal or verified coach numbers only.
  • Students should attend from a shared/visible family space where possible; a parent/guardian may sit in at any time.
  • Coaches never contact students privately outside official academy channels.
  • Class recordings, when made for review purposes, are stored securely and shared only with the enrolled batch.
  • Report any concern immediately to the academy office — every report is treated seriously and confidentially.
Questions about any policy? Write to chesskidoo37@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 90258 46663.
